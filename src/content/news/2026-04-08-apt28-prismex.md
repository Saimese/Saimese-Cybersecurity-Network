## Overview

In the last 24 hours, new details have emerged regarding a sophisticated multi-stage campaign by the Russian state-sponsored threat actor **APT28** (also known as Forest Blizzard, Pawn Storm, or Fancy Bear). The campaign, identified by researchers as **Operation Neusploit**, involves the deployment of a previously undocumented malware suite codenamed **PRISMEX**. 

This campaign is particularly impactful due to its strategic targeting of Ukraine and its NATO allies, specifically focusing on sectors like logistics, maritime, and defense infrastructure.

## Technical Breakdown of PRISMEX

The PRISMEX suite is not a single tool but a collection of interconnected components designed for stealth, persistence, and data exfiltration.

### 1. The Infection Vector: Zero-Day Chaining
APT28 demonstrated advanced knowledge of Microsoft vulnerabilities, weaponizing them before they were publicly disclosed. The attack chain often begins with a spear-phishing email leading to a two-stage exploit:
- **CVE-2026-21509**: A vulnerability that forces the victim's system to retrieve a malicious `.LNK` file.
- **CVE-2026-21513**: An MSHTML bypass that allows the `.LNK` file to execute payloads without triggering security warnings.

### 2. Multi-Stage Execution & Steganography

The suite utilizes advanced steganography to evade network-level detection and security scanning.

#### PrismexSheet (Excel Dropper)
The initial stage often involves a malicious Excel file (`PrismexSheet`). 
- **Decoy**: Displays drone inventory lists or pricing, relevant to the current geopolitical context.
- **Mechanism**: VBA macros extract payloads hidden within the file using steganography.
- **Persistence**: Establishes persistence via **Component Object Model (COM) Hijacking**.

#### PrismexLoader (aka PixyNetLoader)
A critical component in the chain is the `PrismexLoader`, a proxy DLL that manages the loading of the final payload.
- **Algorithm**: It uses a bespoke **"Bit Plane Round Robin"** algorithm to extract a .NET payload scattered across the bit planes of a legitimate-looking image file (`SplashScreen.png`).
- **Memory-Only**: The final payload is executed entirely in memory, leaving a minimal forensic footprint on the disk.

### 3. Persistence: COM Hijacking
The PRISMEX suite heavily relies on **COM Hijacking (T1546.015)**. By modifying the registry keys for legitimate CLSIDs (Class Identifiers), the malware ensures it is loaded by trusted system processes. 
- **PrismexDrop** also utilizes scheduled tasks and COM DLL hijacking to maintain a permanent foothold on the compromised system.

### 4. Command and Control (C2): Cloud Abuse
To blend in with legitimate network traffic, the **PrismexStager** (a customized COVENANT Grunt implant) abuses the **Filen.io** cloud storage service for C2 communication. This "living off the cloud" strategy makes detection via traditional IP/domain blacklisting extremely difficult.

### 5. Capabilities: Espionage and Sabotage
The final payloads delivered by the PRISMEX suite include:
- **MiniDoor**: A specialized backdoor designed to steal Outlook emails and credentials.
- **Destructive Wiper**: In some instances, the COVENANT Grunt payload has been observed executing a wiper command that erases all files in the `%USERPROFILE%` directory, suggesting a dual-purpose capability for both intelligence gathering and operational disruption.

## Impacted Sectors
The campaign has been observed targeting:
- **Ukraine**: Central executive bodies, defense, and emergency services.
- **NATO Allies**: Rail logistics (Poland), maritime/transportation (Romania, Slovenia, Turkey), and ammunition initiative partners (Slovakia, Czech Republic).

## Detection and Mitigation

Defenders should focus on:
1. **Registry Monitoring**: Track unauthorized modifications to `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Classes\CLSID` to detect COM hijacking.
2. **Behavioral Analysis**: Monitor for unusual parent-child process relationships (e.g., Excel spawning `regsvr32.exe` or `rundll32.exe`).
3. **Network Visibility**: Inspect egress traffic to encrypted cloud storage providers (like Filen.io) for anomalies.
4. **Patch Management**: Ensure all Microsoft Office and Windows updates are applied, specifically focusing on the recent 2026 CVEs mentioned.

## Conclusion

The PRISMEX campaign represents a significant escalation in the technical capabilities of APT28. By combining zero-day exploitation, bespoke steganography, and cloud-based C2, the actors have created a highly resilient and stealthy toolkit. The strategic focus on NATO supply chains and logistics indicates a shift toward preparing for potential large-scale operational disruptions.
